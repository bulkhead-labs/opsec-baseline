THE COMPANY

Wren Street

Booking software for physio clinics. Nine people, forty online tools, nobody on security.

  • MayaCo-founder. Runs the company.
  • TheoCo-founder. Runs the product.
  • PriyaOperations. Keeps the tools running.

Last spring, a fake invoice in Wren Street’s name nearly got paid. So they built seven walls, an afternoon or two each.

Their AI assistant did the reading. A person did anything that could lock someone out.

WALL 1: SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE ↓

WALL 1 OF 7 · JUNE

Sign in with Google

Friday is the sales lead’s last day. Priya does the offboarding.

WITHOUT THE WALLThree tools stay open. They were signed up with a personal email.WITH THE WALLOne switch closes most doors. A short list closes the rest.

Turn off one account. When everyone signs in to every tool with their company Google account, that one switch stops a leaver signing in anywhere. A short list closes the few tools that keep them signed in.

You can’t take back access the company never handed out.

Time
Half a day for the admin; 30 minutes per person
You pay for
A Google Workspace Business Starter seat per person

WALL 2 OF 7 · AUGUST

Password manager

A cycling forum Theo forgot about was breached. His old password leaked.

WITHOUT THE WALLSunset2019! on every site. One leak opens them all.WITH THE WALLA different random password on each site. The leak opens only the old forum.

A different password for every site. 1Password makes each one long and random, and fills it in. When one site leaks, that password opens nothing else. Theo remembers one password; 1Password remembers the rest.

Leaks happen on sites you don’t control. Unique passwords keep them there.

Time
2 hours for the admin; 30–60 minutes per person
You pay for
A 1Password Business subscription per person

WALL 3 OF 7 · OCTOBER

Hardware key

Maya gets a perfect fake: “Storage full. Sign in to keep your mail.”

WITHOUT THE WALLPassword and code are just text. The fake page passes both on.WITH THE WALLThe stranger needs her key. The fake gets her password, but the key stays on Maya’s keychain.

The key never leaves your hand. A six-digit code is just text: a fake page can pass it on, and a code kept in the vault falls with the password. A key’s secret can’t be copied, and it answers only the real site.

Anything typed can be passed on. A key has to be in your hand.

Time
2 hours for the admin; 30 minutes per person
You pay for
Two security keys per person (YubiKey 5C NFC)

WALL 4 OF 7 · THE SAME OCTOBER EMAIL

Separate admin accounts

What if that fake had caught the account that runs the company?

WITHOUT THE WALLOne account does everything. Catch it, and you catch the company.WITH THE WALLmaya@ holds no admin. Admin lives on maya-admin@, behind a key.

Two accounts, two jobs. Maya’s everyday account reads mail and holds no admin, so catching it gets a stranger her inbox, not the company. Her admin account signs in only with her key and does nothing else.

Keep the power to change everything off the account that clicks links.

Time
2 hours for the admins; 15 minutes each quarter
You pay for
A Workspace seat per admin account, the spare included, and two keys for the spare

WALL 5 OF 7 · JANUARY

Admin audit

Priya asks the assistant: who is an admin of what?

WITHOUT THE WALL31 admin seats for nine people. One is held by a contractor long gone.WITH THE WALLTwo admins per tool. The assistant drafts the table; Priya cuts it, one seat at a time.

List every admin, then cut. Admin rights pile up, on people who left and on tools nobody checks. The audit keeps each role to the few who need it, and is checked again each quarter.

Every extra admin is one more account worth stealing.

Time
2–3 hours for an admin, with the assistant; 30 minutes each quarter
You pay for
Nothing to buy, unless a smaller Google role needs another admin seat

WALL 6 OF 7 · MARCH

Phone number lock

A caller tells Theo’s carrier: “I lost my phone. Move my number.”

WITHOUT THE WALLHis number moves to a stranger. So does every text-message code.WITH THE WALLNo PIN, no move. The number stays put until Theo unlocks it himself.

Lock your number at the carrier. A caller who talks your carrier into moving it gets every text-message code meant for you. A number lock and a SIM lock stop the move. Accounts that matter sign in with keys, not texts.

A phone number was never meant to prove who you are.

Time
30 minutes for an admin; 20–30 minutes per person
You pay for
Nothing to buy: settings on plans people already have

WALL 7 OF 7 · MAY

AI connectors

Priya’s assistant drafts her replies. One email hid an instruction for it.

WITHOUT THE WALLIt does what the hidden text says. With access to everything, it sends out every clinic’s records.WITH THE WALLGoogle locks it out of Drive. A rule in the AI tool stops it sending.

Give it only what its job needs. An assistant acts on what it reads, hidden text included: prompt injection. Google can lock it out of Drive. Gmail has no “drafts only” permission, so “don’t send” is a rule in the AI tool, not a lock.

The assistant isn’t the problem. What it can reach is.

Time
2–3 hours for an admin, with the assistant; 15 minutes per person; 30 minutes each quarter
You pay for
Nothing to buy; some AI tools need a business plan for these settings
