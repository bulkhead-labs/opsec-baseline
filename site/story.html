Seven Walls

A ship doesn’t sink because water gets in. It sinks when nothing stops the water.

WITHOUT THE WALLNo walls inside: one breach floods the whole hull.WITH THE WALLWalls inside: the water stays in one compartment.

This is one small company’s year, and the seven walls that held.

MEET WREN STREET ↓

THE COMPANY

Wren Street

Booking software for physio clinics. Nine people, forty online tools, nobody on security.

  • MayaCo-founder. Runs the company.
  • TheoCo-founder. Runs the product.
  • PriyaOperations. Keeps the tools running.

Last spring, a fake invoice in Wren Street’s name nearly got paid. So they built seven walls, an afternoon or two each.

Their AI assistant did the reading. A person did anything that could lock someone out.

WALL 1: SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE ↓

WALL 1 OF 7 · JUNE

Sign in with Google

Friday is the sales lead’s last day. Priya does the offboarding.

WITHOUT THE WALLTools signed up with a personal email stay open after they leave.WITH THE WALLOne switch closes most doors. A short list closes the rest.

You can’t take back access the company never handed out.

Time
Half a day for the admin; 30 minutes per person
You pay for
A Google Workspace Business Starter seat per person

WALL 2 OF 7 · AUGUST

Password manager

A cycling forum Theo forgot about was breached. His old password leaked.

WITHOUT THE WALLOne password, reused. The leak opens every door it fits.WITH THE WALLA different password for every site. The leak opens one old forum.

Leaks happen on sites you don’t control. Unique passwords keep them there.

Time
2 hours for the admin; 30–60 minutes per person
You pay for
A 1Password Business subscription per person
Read the module → NEXT: HARDWARE KEY ↓

WALL 3 OF 7 · OCTOBER

Hardware key

Maya gets a perfect fake: “Storage full. Sign in to keep your mail.”

WITHOUT THE WALLThe fake page passes her password and her “Yes” straight to Google.WITH THE WALLThe key checks the address. On the real site it answers. On a look-alike it stays dark.

Good people fall for good fakes. A key doesn’t need anyone to spot them.

Time
2 hours for the admin; 30 minutes per person
You pay for
Two security keys per person (YubiKey 5C NFC)
Read the module → NEXT: SEPARATE ADMIN ACCOUNTS ↓

WALL 4 OF 7 · THE SAME OCTOBER EMAIL

Separate admin accounts

What if that fake had caught the account that runs the company?

WITHOUT THE WALLHer everyday account was also the top admin. One slip reaches every account.WITH THE WALLAdmin lives on separate, key-only accounts. Her everyday account can’t change the company.

Keep the power to change everything off the account that clicks links.

Time
2 hours for the admins; 15 minutes each quarter
You pay for
A Workspace seat per admin account, the spare included, and two keys for the spare
Read the module → NEXT: ADMIN AUDIT ↓

WALL 5 OF 7 · JANUARY

Admin audit

Priya asks the assistant: who is an admin of what?

WITHOUT THE WALL31 admin seats for nine people, one held by a contractor long gone.WITH THE WALLThe assistant drafts the table. Priya cuts it to two per tool, one at a time.

Every extra admin is one more account worth stealing.

Time
2–3 hours for an admin, with the assistant; 30 minutes each quarter
You pay for
Nothing to buy, unless a smaller Google role needs another admin seat
Read the module → NEXT: PHONE NUMBER LOCK ↓

WALL 6 OF 7 · MARCH

Phone number lock

A caller tells Theo’s carrier: “I lost my phone. Move my number.”

WITHOUT THE WALLThe carrier moves his number. Every text-message code now goes to a stranger.WITH THE WALLNo PIN, no move. The number stays put until Theo unlocks it himself.

A phone number was never meant to prove who you are.

Time
30 minutes for an admin; 20–30 minutes per person
You pay for
Nothing to buy: settings on plans people already have
Read the module → NEXT: AI CONNECTORS ↓

WALL 7 OF 7 · MAY

AI connectors

Priya’s assistant drafts her replies. One email hid a line meant for it.

WITHOUT THE WALLWith access to everything, nothing stops the hidden line. The invoices go out.WITH THE WALLIt can read and draft. Drive is locked; sending is switched off in the tool.

The assistant isn’t the problem. What it can reach is.

Time
2–3 hours for an admin, with the assistant; 15 minutes per person; 30 minutes each quarter
You pay for
Nothing to buy; some AI tools need a business plan for these settings
