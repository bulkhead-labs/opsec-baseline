THE COMPANY

Wren Street

Booking software for physio clinics. Nine people, forty online tools, nobody on security.

  • MayaCo-founder. Runs the company.
  • TheoCo-founder. Runs the product.
  • PriyaOperations. Keeps the tools running.

Last spring, a fake invoice in Wren Street’s name nearly got paid. So they built seven walls, an afternoon or two each.

Their AI assistant did the reading. A person did anything that could lock someone out.

WALL 1: SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE ↓

WALL 1 OF 7 · JUNE

Sign in with Google

Friday is the sales lead’s last day. Priya does the offboarding.

WITHOUT THE WALLTools signed up with a personal email stay open after they leave.WITH THE WALLOne switch closes most doors. A short list closes the rest.

Everyone signs in to every tool with their company Google account. When someone leaves, turning off that one account stops them signing in anywhere. A short list closes the few tools that keep them signed in.

You can’t take back access the company never handed out.

Time
Half a day for the admin; 30 minutes per person
You pay for
A Google Workspace Business Starter seat per person

WALL 2 OF 7 · AUGUST

Password manager

A cycling forum Theo forgot about was breached. His old password leaked.

WITHOUT THE WALLThe same password, Sunset2019!, on every site. One leak opens them all.WITH THE WALLA different random password on each site. The leak opens only the old forum.

A password manager makes a long, random password for every site and fills it in for you. When one site leaks, that password opens nothing else. Theo remembers one password; 1Password remembers the rest.

Leaks happen on sites you don’t control. Unique passwords keep them there.

Time
2 hours for the admin; 30–60 minutes per person
You pay for
A 1Password Business subscription per person

WALL 3 OF 7 · OCTOBER

Hardware key

Maya gets a perfect fake: “Storage full. Sign in to keep your mail.”

WITHOUT THE WALLHer password and her six-digit code are just text. The fake page passes both on.WITH THE WALLThe fake gets her password. To use it, the stranger needs a key on Maya’s keychain.

A six-digit code is just text: a fake page can pass it on, and a code saved beside the password in a vault falls with it. A hardware key stays on your keychain. Its secret can’t be copied, and it answers only the real site.

Anything typed can be passed on. A key has to be in your hand.

Time
2 hours for the admin; 30 minutes per person
You pay for
Two security keys per person (YubiKey 5C NFC)

WALL 4 OF 7 · THE SAME OCTOBER EMAIL

Separate admin accounts

What if that fake had caught the account that runs the company?

WITHOUT THE WALLOne account reads mail and runs the company. Catch it, and you catch everything.WITH THE WALLEveryday mail lives on maya@, which holds no admin. Admin lives on maya-admin@, behind a key.

Maya has two accounts. Her everyday one reads mail, opens links and holds no admin, so catching it gets a stranger her inbox, not the company. Her admin one signs in only with her key and does nothing else.

Keep the power to change everything off the account that clicks links.

Time
2 hours for the admins; 15 minutes each quarter
You pay for
A Workspace seat per admin account, the spare included, and two keys for the spare

WALL 5 OF 7 · JANUARY

Admin audit

Priya asks the assistant: who is an admin of what?

WITHOUT THE WALL31 admin seats for nine people, one held by a contractor long gone.WITH THE WALLThe assistant drafts the table. Priya cuts it to two per tool, one at a time.

Admin rights pile up: on people who left, and on tools nobody checks. The audit lists who is an admin of every tool, keeps it to the few who need it, and is checked again each quarter.

Every extra admin is one more account worth stealing.

Time
2–3 hours for an admin, with the assistant; 30 minutes each quarter
You pay for
Nothing to buy, unless a smaller Google role needs another admin seat

WALL 6 OF 7 · MARCH

Phone number lock

A caller tells Theo’s carrier: “I lost my phone. Move my number.”

WITHOUT THE WALLThe carrier moves his number. Every text-message code now goes to a stranger.WITH THE WALLNo PIN, no move. The number stays put until Theo unlocks it himself.

A caller who talks your carrier into moving your number gets every text-message code meant for you. A number lock and a SIM lock stop the move. The accounts that matter sign in with keys, not texts, so a number opens nothing.

A phone number was never meant to prove who you are.

Time
30 minutes for an admin; 20–30 minutes per person
You pay for
Nothing to buy: settings on plans people already have

WALL 7 OF 7 · MAY

AI connectors

Priya’s assistant drafts her replies. One email hid a line meant for it.

WITHOUT THE WALLWith access to everything, the assistant follows the hidden line. The invoices go out.WITH THE WALLGoogle locks it out of Drive. A setting in the AI tool stops it sending.

Give an assistant only what its job needs: here, reading mail and writing drafts. Google can lock it out of Drive. Gmail has no “drafts only” permission, so “don’t send” is a setting in the AI tool: a rule, not a lock.

The assistant isn’t the problem. What it can reach is.

Time
2–3 hours for an admin, with the assistant; 15 minutes per person; 30 minutes each quarter
You pay for
Nothing to buy; some AI tools need a business plan for these settings
